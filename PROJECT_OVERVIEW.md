# PRIORA &mdash; Enterprise Hackathon Product Overview

## Product Name
**PRIORA**

## Tagline
*"Intelligent Work Orchestration"*

## Value Proposition
*"Employees shouldn't have to spend mental energy deciding what to work on next, while managers shouldn't have to discover execution problems after they become critical."*

---

## 1. Product Strategy & Vision

Enterprise organizations lose billions of dollars annually to disconnected workflows, repetitive status meetings, and undetected operational bottlenecks. 

Existing project management tools (Jira, Asana, Monday) are **passive filing cabinets**: they require users to manually drag cards and guess which task among 10 is actually the most urgent. Meanwhile, managers discover execution stalls only during weekly sprint retrospectives when delivery dates have already slipped.

**PRIORA transforms project management from a passive tracking tool into an active, intelligent orchestration system.**

---

## 2. Core Personas

### Persona A: The Individual Contributor (Employee)
- **Example**: Rahul Sharma, Senior Integration Engineer (Operations)
- **Daily Friction**: Receives 10 assigned tasks. Needs to know what to start right now without guessing.
- **Priora Experience**:
  - Opens `/employee/dashboard`.
  - Sees the **Next Best Action**: *Resolve Payment API Integration*.
  - Immediately understands why (due today, critical SLA impact, blocks 3 downstream tasks).
  - Clicks **START TASK**. If blocked, marks it blocked with one click to trigger managerial assistance.

### Persona B: The Engineering & Operations Leader (Manager)
- **Example**: Sarah Chen, VP of Engineering & Operations
- **Daily Friction**: Blind to team slowdowns and priority deviations until releases fail.
- **Priora Experience**:
  - Opens `/management/dashboard`.
  - Sees **Team Health**: 4 teams are green, but Operations is 🟠 *Needs Attention* (68% vs 82% expected).
  - Sees **Attention Center**: Priority deviation detected (Aman started Task #3 before recommended Task #2).
  - Asks the **AI Management Assistant**: *"Why is Operations behind?"* and receives an actionable data-backed summary.
  - Resolves upstream vendor bottlenecks before deadlines are breached.

---

## 3. Product Differentiators

| Feature | Legacy Tools (Jira / Asana) | PRIORA Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| **Task Ordering** | Manual drag-and-drop / arbitrary | **Autonomous AI-ranked Recommended Execution Order** |
| **Daily Starting Point** | Overwhelming list of 10+ items | **Single Flagship "Next Best Action" Card** |
| **Ranking Rationale** | None (opaque priority label) | **Explainable Business Impact Factors** |
| **Problem Detection** | Retroactive status reports | **Real-Time Early Warning System & Priority Adherence** |
| **Management Visibility**| Burn-down charts showing past failure | **Predictive Bottleneck & Downstream Delay Alerts** |
| **Surveillance Policy** | Often combines invasive employee tracking | **100% Non-invasive; strictly evaluates work telemetry** |

---

## 4. Business Impact & ROI
1. **Saves 30–45 minutes per employee daily**: Eliminates daily prioritization fatigue and guessing.
2. **Reduces Critical Blockers by 70%**: Downstream tasks are flagged the moment an upstream task is blocked.
3. **Eliminates Unnecessary Status Meetings**: Managers have continuous live visibility into team health deltas.
